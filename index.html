Click the game, then: WASD to walk · Mouse to look · Shift to run · Space to jump · Hold C to crouch (in the sea: C dives, Space comes up) · V: lock his facing · R: reset the view · T: automatic fishing pole (on the fishing boat) · Q: gun (1/2/3 to switch, 7 for your camo rifle) · E: use or buy · T: talk to someone (type anything) · Hold G: strangle someone next to you · K: knife (left mouse to stab) · X: kick · Z: throw someone down · 4/5/6: smoke, drink, eat · Right mouse: aim · Left mouse: shoot, or punch with no gun out · F: get in/out of a car (or pull a driver out of one stopped in the road), a boat or a jet ski (WASD drive, Space brake; in a boat W/S set the throttle) · Esc to free the mouse
